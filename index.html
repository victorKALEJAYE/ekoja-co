The Firm

A dynamic, dedicated team of legal practitioners.

“The essence of legal practice is to serve and protect the interests of our clients.”

O.J. Ekoja & Co. is a duly registered Nigerian law firm with its principal office in the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja. We are known for our commitment to excellence, prompt service delivery and the highest standards of professional integrity.

That founding principle shapes how we work. We combine practical legal solutions, strategic thinking and innovative counsel to meet the varied needs of our clients, from individuals and families to growing businesses and established corporations.

Under the leadership of our Principal Counsel, Oche Ekoja, Esq., the firm pairs technical legal competence with a client-centred approach. Effective representation demands more than knowing the law. It demands attentiveness, accessibility and a clear understanding of what each client is trying to achieve.

We place a premium on responsiveness. In legal and commercial transactions time is often of the essence, so we deliver prompt, well-considered advice without compromising the quality of our work.
