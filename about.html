About O.J. Ekoja & Co.

O.J. Ekoja & Co. is a duly registered law firm comprising a team of dynamic, innovative and dedicated legal practitioners, with its principal office in the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja, Nigeria. The firm is distinguished by its commitment to excellence, prompt service delivery and the highest standards of professional integrity.

Our practice is founded on the understanding that the essence of legal practice is to serve and protect the interests of clients. This philosophy informs our approach to legal service delivery, combining practical legal solutions, strategic thinking and innovative counsel to address the diverse needs of our clientele.

With our principal office in Abuja and an extensive network of professional relationships across the six geopolitical zones of Nigeria, we are positioned to provide legal support and representation across different jurisdictions.

Under the leadership of our Principal Counsel, Oche Ekoja, Esq., the firm brings together technical legal competence and a client-centred approach to service delivery. We recognise that effective legal representation requires not only a sound understanding of the law but also attentiveness, accessibility and a clear appreciation of each client’s unique objectives.

At O.J. Ekoja & Co., we place a premium on responsiveness and timely delivery, recognising that in legal and commercial transactions, time is often of the essence. We are committed to providing prompt, effective and well-considered legal solutions without compromising the quality of our work.

We also recognise that effective communication is fundamental to a successful solicitor-client relationship. We use both conventional and electronic channels to stay accessible and keep clients informed throughout their instructions, and we welcome client feedback to keep improving the quality, efficiency and responsiveness of our services.
